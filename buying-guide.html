Buying guide & FAQs

Everything you need to know about buying land or a home with Horic Homes, from your first enquiry to receiving your documents.

Step by Step

The buying process

  1. Enquiry

    Contact our sales team by phone, WhatsApp or through the website. We will send details of suitable properties, current prices and payment options.

    Handled by: Sales & Client Services

  2. Site inspection

    Visit the property with a member of our team. Saturday tours run every week, and weekday visits can be arranged. If you are abroad, we can arrange a live video inspection.

    Handled by: Sales & Client Services

  3. Document review

    We give you copies of the site plan, the Lands Commission search report and the title documents. We encourage you to have your own lawyer review them and to carry out an independent search.

    Handled by: Legal & Documentation

  4. Sale agreement

    You sign a sale agreement setting out the price, payment schedule, fees, and the terms for cancellation and refunds.

    Handled by: Legal & Documentation

  5. Payment

    Pay into the Horic Homes corporate bank account, in full or by instalment. An official receipt is issued for every payment.

    Handled by: Finance

  6. Demarcation & documents

    Your plot is pillared on site, and your indenture is prepared and executed in your name. For houses, keys are handed over at completion.

    Handled by: Survey & Planning, Legal & Documentation

  7. Title registration

    We support the registration of your title at the Lands Commission and keep you updated until it is complete.

    Handled by: Legal & Documentation

Protect yourself from land fraud

Horic Homes will never ask you to pay into a personal bank or mobile money account. All payments are made to our registered corporate account and confirmed in writing. If anyone asks you to pay elsewhere in our name, call our office on 054 800 0393 before you pay.

Documents

The documents you should receive

Keep the originals in a safe place and give copies to your lawyer.

Site plan

A plan of your plot prepared and signed by a licensed surveyor, showing its dimensions, boundaries and coordinates. You need it for searches, the indenture and your building permit.

Search report

Issued by the Lands Commission, it shows who holds an interest in the land and whether there are any registered disputes, mortgages or other claims.

Indenture

The legal document that transfers the land to you, signed by the grantor and witnesses. It should state the lease term and carry your site plan.

Land title certificate

Issued by the Lands Commission once your interest is registered. It is the strongest evidence of your ownership.

Official receipts

Issued by Horic Homes for every payment, on company letterhead and referencing your sale agreement.

Building permit

Not issued by us, but required before you build. Apply to the Municipal or District Assembly with your site plan and building drawings.

Due Diligence

Conducting a search at the Lands Commission

A search confirms who holds an interest in a parcel of land and whether there are any registered claims against it. You can request one at the Lands Commission office for the region where the land is located.

  • Submit a search application with a copy of the site plan.
  • Pay the prescribed search fee and keep your receipt.
  • Collect the search report when it is ready.

We include a recent search report with every property we sell. You or your lawyer are welcome to carry out your own.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What documents will I receive?

For land, you receive an indenture in your name, a site plan prepared by a licensed surveyor, a copy of the Lands Commission search report and official receipts for all payments. For houses and apartments you also receive the sale or sublease agreement. We then support the registration of your title at the Lands Commission.

Can I carry out my own search before buying?

Yes, and we recommend it. We will give you the site plan so that you or your lawyer can conduct an independent search at the Lands Commission before you pay.

Can I buy if I live outside Ghana?

Yes. Many of our clients are Ghanaians living abroad. Inspections can be done by live video call or by a representative you appoint. Documents can be signed through a lawyer holding your power of attorney, and payments are made by bank transfer to our corporate account.

Can non-Ghanaians buy land?

Yes, on a leasehold basis. Under Article 266 of the 1992 Constitution, non-citizens may hold land in Ghana on leases of up to 50 years. Our team and your lawyer will explain the terms that apply to you.

How long is the lease?

Most residential land in Ghana is held on leasehold. The lease term for each property is stated in the sale agreement and the indenture. Ask our sales team for the term that applies to the property you are interested in.

Do you offer instalment payments?

Yes, on most land schemes and some homes. The deposit, duration and schedule are set out in your sale agreement. Plans run up to 24 months depending on the scheme. Late or missed payments are handled under the terms of the agreement.

When is my plot demarcated?

Your plot is pillared on site once it is fully paid for. On instalment plans, the plot is reserved in your name from the date of your first payment.

How soon can I start building?

Once your plot is paid for in full and demarcated. You will need a building permit from the relevant Municipal or District Assembly before construction begins.

Do you charge agency or documentation fees?

Any fees on top of the purchase price, such as documentation or registration fees, are listed in writing before you commit. There are no hidden charges.

What happens if I change my mind?

Cancellation and refund terms are set out in the sale agreement you sign. Please read them carefully, and ask our team to explain anything that is unclear before you pay.
