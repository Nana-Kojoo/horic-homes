Buying Process

How buying with Horic Homes works

The same documented process for every buyer, whether you are in Ghana or abroad.

Read the Buying Guide

  1. Enquiry & site visit

    Speak to our sales team and inspect the property in person, or by video call if you are abroad.

  2. Document review

    We give you the site plan, search report and title documents. Your lawyer can review them independently.

  3. Agreement & payment

    Sign a sale agreement and pay into our corporate account, outright or by instalment.

  4. Documentation & handover

    Your indenture is prepared in your name, the plot is demarcated, and we support title registration.

Protect yourself from land fraud

Horic Homes will never ask you to pay into a personal bank or mobile money account. All payments are made to our registered corporate account and confirmed in writing. If anyone asks you to pay elsewhere in our name, call our office on 054 800 0393 before you pay.
