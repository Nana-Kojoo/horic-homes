Investors & partners

We work with individual investors, diaspora groups, corporate buyers, landowners and developers. Every engagement is documented under a formal agreement.

Why Residential Land

Demand for documented land

Ghana has a long-standing shortage of housing, and it is most acute around Accra, Tema and Kumasi. As these cities grow outwards, land on established transport routes is steadily absorbed into new residential areas.

Buyers are most willing to commit to land whose ownership is clear and properly documented. That is where Horic Homes focuses: we acquire land, resolve the documentation, and bring it to market in a form that buyers and lenders can rely on.

Engagement Process

How an engagement works

  1. Introductory meeting

    We discuss your objectives, budget and timeline with our investor relations team.

  2. Due diligence

    We share title documents, site plans and search reports, and arrange site inspections for you and your advisers.

  3. Agreement

    Terms are set out in a written agreement, reviewed by both parties’ lawyers before signing.

  4. Execution & reporting

    Funds go to our corporate account only. Partners receive regular written progress updates.
