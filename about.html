About Horic Homes

A Ghanaian real estate company that acquires, documents and sells residential land and homes.

Who We Are

Built on proper documentation

Horic Homes acquires land from landowners, surveys and documents it, and sells residential plots and homes to individuals, families and organisations. Our properties are in Greater Accra and the Eastern, Central and Ashanti regions.

Land disputes and multiple sales are still a serious risk for buyers in Ghana. Our work starts long before a property is advertised. We confirm ownership with landowners and traditional authorities, conduct searches at the Lands Commission, and have every parcel surveyed by licensed professionals.

Horic Homes is a member of the Horic Group.

Purpose & Values

What guides our work

Our mission

To give buyers in Ghana and abroad secure, documented access to land and homes, at fair prices and with clear terms.

Our vision

To be the most trusted name in residential land in Ghana, known for clean titles and transparent dealings.

Integrity

We only sell what we can document, and we say so plainly when something is still in progress.

Transparency

Prices, payment terms and documents are set out in writing before any money changes hands.

Accountability

Every payment is receipted, and every sale is handled by more than one department.

Service

We stay in contact with buyers until documents are delivered and the property is handed over.

How we are organised

Each stage of a sale is handled by a dedicated department, so no single person controls a transaction. Meet the team.

Land Acquisition

Identifies land, engages landowners and traditional authorities, and confirms ownership before purchase.

Survey & Planning

Works with licensed surveyors on site plans, layouts, demarcation and planning approvals.

Legal & Documentation

Handles searches, sale agreements, indentures and title registration with our legal advisers.

Sales & Client Services

Handles enquiries, site tours and buyer support from first contact through to handover.

Finance & Investor Relations

Manages payments, receipts and instalment accounts, and is the contact point for investors.
