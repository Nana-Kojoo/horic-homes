Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: November 2026

Horic Homes Ltd. (“Horic Homes”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Information we collect

  • Details you give us through our website, by phone, by WhatsApp or by email: your name, phone number, email address and the content of your enquiry.
  • Information needed to complete a purchase, such as identification documents, addresses and payment records.
  • Basic technical information that your browser sends when you visit our website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and arrange site visits.
  • To prepare sale agreements, receipts, indentures and other documents.
  • To tell you about properties that match your requirements, where you have asked us to.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

WhatsApp enquiries

Our website forms open WhatsApp with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with people who need it to complete your transaction, such as our legal advisers, licensed surveyors and the Lands Commission, or where the law requires us to.

Retention and security

We keep transaction records for as long as the law requires, and enquiry records for no longer than necessary. Access to personal information is limited to staff who need it for their work.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop sending you marketing messages. Contact us at info@horichomes.com or call 054 800 0393.
